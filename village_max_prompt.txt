## 💡 User Core Pain Points & Requirements (Logged on 23-Nov-2026)

### 1. 🏆 Already Maxed Items ("Yethulam already maxed aaiduchu nu therila")
- **Need:** Dedicated "Already Maxed" filter/section or glowing MAX badge counter.
- **Display:** Total count of maxed items (e.g. `14 Items Maxed`) + list of fully maxed troops/spells/heroes/equipment.

### 2. ⚡ Almost Maxed ("Yethalam max aagurathuku romba kitta irukku - Quick Wins")
- **Need:** "Almost Maxed / Low Hanging Fruits" filter (Items needing only 1 or 2 levels to become 100% MAX).
- **Benefit:** User can finish these first with 1-2 upgrades to get immediate permanent max power.

### 3. ⏳ Dynamic Real-Time Target Date for EACH Individual Item (🔥 DAILY LIVE AUTO-UPDATE)
- **Requirement:** "Each item ku target date daily um update aaganum, yellame realtime la update aaganum."
- **Execution Math:**
  - Inniku date + `(remaining levels * avg upgrade days)`.
  - Daily automatic live date projection for EVERY Hero, Defense, Troop, and Equipment!
  - Example:
    - *Barbarian King (Lvl 56/115):* `59 levels left` ➔ `ETA: Oct 14, 2027 (~325 Days remaining)`.
    - *Archer Queen (Lvl 64/115):* `51 levels left` ➔ `ETA: Aug 02, 2027 (~252 Days remaining)`.
    - *Grand Warden (Lvl 38/90):* `52 levels left` ➔ `ETA: Aug 12, 2027 (~262 Days remaining)`.
    - *Battle Machine (Lvl 28/35):* `7 levels left` ➔ `ETA: Dec 28, 2026 (~35 Days remaining - 6th Builder unlocked!)`.
  - Date dynamically recalculates every time page is opened or refreshed!

### 4. 📅 Overall Village 100% Max Date (Daily Dynamic Projected Finish Line)
- **Need:** Top-level Hero Max ETA & Overall Village ETA with realistic builder allocation:
  - Total Remaining Days: dynamically computed from remaining levels.
  - Target Calendar Date: Live JS Date object formatting (e.g. `Target Completion: May 28, 2027`).

### 5. 💰 Loot Strategy & 💎 Gem Strategy ("Loot eppadi fast-ah edukuradhu & Gems eppadi spend pannanum?")
- **Need:** Dedicated 1-Tap Strategy cards inside Village Max:
  - **Loot Strategy:** Sneaky Goblin + Super Wall Breaker farming guide with minimum trophy league (Masters II / Champions III).
  - **Gem Spending Hierarchy:** 5th Builder purchase > Book of Heroes in Trader (500 gems) > NEVER spend gems on instant resource filling.

### 6. ⚔️ War Play Guidance ("War eppadi play pannanum?")
- **Need:** Quick War Readiness status based on current base:
  - If 2+ Heroes are upgrading: Auto warn "Upgrade War Mode - Opt Out or Safe 2-Star Attack".
  - Meta War Armies for TH18 (Root Rider Smash, Super Archer Blimp).

### 7. 💎 Gem Mine & Obstacles Max Tracker ("Gem mine full aaiducha & Obstacles limit?")
- **Need:** Gem & Passive Resource Economy tracker:
  - Builder Base Gem Mine max storage reminder (Lvl 10 Gem mine: 5.4 gems/day, cap = 26 gems).
  - Obstacles 45-limit rule (clear obstacles every 4 days for free 6-12 gems).

### 8. 🧪 Laboratory Upgrade Priority ("Lab-la yethai muthalla max pannanum?")
- **Need:** Clear Lab Meta Tier List:
  - **Tier S (Must Max First):** Root Rider, Healers, Freeze, Rage.
  - **Tier A:** Dragons, Balloons, Overgrowth Spell.
  - **Tier B:** Situational troops.

### 9. 🛖 Helpers Hut (Apprentice Builder) Strategy & ROI
- **Apprentice Builder:** Permanent daily builder speedup. Longest upgrades-la mattum podanum (Town Hall, Monolith, Eagle, High-level Heroes).
- **Gems Upgrade:** Level 1 to 3 is maximum value (saves 45-90 builder days/year).

### 10. 🔮 Ores System Mastery (Shiny, Glowy, Starry Ores & Equipment Strategy)
- **The 3 Ores:** Shiny (daily common), Glowy (bottleneck for milestone levels), Starry (Wars/CWL for Epic equipment).
- **Must-Equip Combos:**
  - Barbarian King: Giant Gauntlet + Rage Vial / Spiky Ball.
  - Archer Queen: Frozen Arrow + Invisibility Vial / Healer Puppet.
  - Grand Warden: Eternal Tome + Healing Tome.
  - Royal Champion: Haste Vial + Rocket Spear.
