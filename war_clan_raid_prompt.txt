================================================================================
1. CORE OBJECTIVE & USER PAIN POINTS
================================================================================
In Clash of Clans, competitive clan progression revolves around 3 major modes:
1. Classic Clan Wars (War Stars, Win Streaks, Loot Bonus, War Frequency).
2. Clan War League / CWL (Tier, Medals, Roster Selection, Performance Stars, Shop Priority).
3. Clan Capital Raid Weekends (Raid Medals, Capital Gold, District Destructions, Trader Strategy).

Currently, in-game UI hides vital strategic metrics:
- Players don't know exact total war stars contributed vs potential stars.
- CWL medal yield vs shop buying priority (Hammer of Building vs Hammer of Fighting).
- Raid medals wasted on wrong items instead of Research Potions / Pet Potions.
- Clan members' attack compliance (who missed attacks, who got 3 stars).

================================================================================
2. DETAILED FEATURE BREAKDOWN & ARCHITECTURE
================================================================================

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
PART A: CLAN WAR MASTERY (CLASSIC WARS)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Live Clan War Status:
  * War State: inWar / preparation / warEnded / notInWar.
  * Opponent Clan Name, Level, Badge & Score.
  * Live Star Comparison: [Our Clan Stars] vs [Opponent Clan Stars].
  * Total Destruction Percentage (%) comparison.
  * Real-time War Timer Countdown (Remaining Battle Time / Preparation Time).
- Player War Performance Card:
  * Total Career War Stars earned (API: player.warStars).
  * Attacks used (e.g., 2/2 or 1/2 or 0/2 attacks left).
  * 3-Star Success Rate across recent wars.
  * Recommended targets based on player Town Hall 18 status and hero levels.
- Clan War Log & Streaks:
  * Current War Win Streak.
  * Total Wars Won, Lost, Tied.
  * Public War Log toggle check (if war log is public in API).

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
PART B: CLAN WAR LEAGUE (CWL) - ELITE LEAGUE HUB
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- League Tier & Standings:
  * Clan Current CWL League (e.g., Master I, Champion III, Crystal I).
  * Group Standings Table (8 clans ranked 1st to 8th by Stars + Destruction %).
  * Promotion / Demotion Zone indicators (Top 1-2 green promotion, Bottom 2 red demotion).
- CWL Stars & Bonus Medal Calculator:
  * Player CWL Star Tracker (1 to 8 stars threshold for full medal rewards).
  * Exact CWL Medals Earned based on placement & clan tier.
  * Clan Leader / Co-leader Bonus Medals allocation recommendation algorithm.
- CWL League Shop Priority Engine:
  * Hammer of Building (Best value: 120 Medals for 16d TH18 defense upgrade).
  * Hammer of Fighting / Spells (120 Medals for 14d lab research).
  * Hammer of Heroes (165 Medals - comparison vs Builder Potion value).
  * Exclusive CWL Sceneries & Champion Hero Skins tracker.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
PART C: CLAN CAPITAL RAID WEEKENDS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Raid Weekend Live Status:
  * Raid State: Ongoing (Friday 07:00 UTC to Monday 07:00 UTC) / Ended.
  * Live Raid Weekend countdown timer.
  * Total Capital Gold looted by Clan this weekend.
  * Total Enemy Districts 100% destroyed.
- Player Raid Performance:
  * Attacks Used: [6 / 6] (including +1 bonus attack for 3-star district finish).
  * Total Capital Gold contributed to upgrades.
  * Estimated Raid Medals payout (typically 1,100 - 1,500+ medals per weekend).
- Raid Medals Trader Optimization Guide:
  * Tier S Priority: 3x Research Potions (600 Medals = 3 days off Lab time).
  * Tier A Priority: 3x Pet Potions (when Pet House is bottleneck).
  * Tier B Priority: Wall Rings / Clock Tower Potions (Builder Base).
  * Anti-Waste Rule: Never spend Raid Medals on standard Gold/Elixir resources.

3. API DATA SOURCES & CONTRACT
================================================================================
- Player Endpoint: `/players/{playerTag}`
  * `warStars`, `warPreference`, `clan.tag`, `clan.name`, `townHallLevel`
- Current War Endpoint: `/clans/{clanTag}/currentwar`
  * `state`, `teamSize`, `attacksPerMember`, `battleEndTime`, `clan`, `opponent`
- War Log Endpoint: `/clans/{clanTag}/warlog`
  * Historical war outcomes, win streaks, destruction %
- CWL Group Endpoint: `/clans/{clanTag}/currentwarleaguegroup`
  * `season`, `clans`, `rounds`, `wars`
- Capital Raid Seasons Endpoint: `/clans/{clanTag}/capitalraidseasons`
  * `state`, `startTime`, `endTime`, `capitalTotalLoot`, `raidsCompleted`, `members`
